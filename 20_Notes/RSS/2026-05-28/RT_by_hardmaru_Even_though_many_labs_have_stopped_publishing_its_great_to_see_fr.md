## Inhalt

Even though many labs have stopped publishing, it’s great to see frontier research is alive and well. This one 👇🏼 from @hardmaru at @SakanaAILabs hardmaru (@hardmaru) For over a decade, we’ve accepted that end-to-end backprop is the only way to train deep networks. But holding the entire network in memory all at once is why AI training is hitting a resource wall. We found a new way to break the network into blocks and train them independently. The trick? Treating the network’s forward pass like a diffusion model denoising a signal. This reinterpretation slashes the memory needed to train deep models. In our #ICLR2026 paper ( arxiv.org/abs/2506.14202 ), we matched end-to-end performance across ViTs, DiTs, and LLMs. We did this while training just one isolated block at a time. — http://nitter.perennialte.ch/hardmaru/status/2059648995132367277#m
