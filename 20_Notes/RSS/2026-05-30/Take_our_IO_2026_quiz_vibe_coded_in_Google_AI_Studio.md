Heads up, builders and devs: You can test your knowledge of  (https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/ai/google-io-2026-all-our-announcements/) our I/O 2026 announcements with this  (https://ai.studio/apps/bundled/google_io_2026_quiz?fullscreenApplet=true) vibe coded quiz built in Google AI Studio.

In case you missed it, at  (https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/developers-tools/google-io-2026-collection/) Google I/O 2026, we announced  (https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/developers-tools/google-ai-studio-io-2026/) new ways and places you can use Google AI Studio. With Google AI Studio, powered by the Antigravity coding agent, you can bring ideas to life with the latest Gemini models even if you’re not a seasoned developer.

As a case in point, I vibe coded this quiz myself — and I'm an editor with zero coding background, not a developer. To make the quiz, I used  (https://gemini.google.com/) Gemini to generate a prompt for Google AI Studio to build the exact quiz I envisioned. I uploaded sources including announcements and design inspiration. Gemini delivered a helpful, creative, highly specific prompt I took to Google AI Studio. I refined the prompt based on the previews, and added the actual quiz text.

Check out the quiz to get to know Google AI Studio, then  (http://aistudio.google.com/apps) try building something yourself.
