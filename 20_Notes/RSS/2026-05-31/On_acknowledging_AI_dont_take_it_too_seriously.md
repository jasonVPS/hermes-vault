On acknowledging AI (don’t take it too seriously) Ethan Mollick (@emollick) My modest proposal for how to acknowledge AI work is to use the Latin phase "Fieri Iussit," which means "commanded to be made," a common phrase on Roman Empire buildings. You didn't make the thing, but you commanded it be done, so the acknowledgement is "Ego hoc fieri iussi." — http://nitter.perennialte.ch/emollick/status/1969993499157446943#m
