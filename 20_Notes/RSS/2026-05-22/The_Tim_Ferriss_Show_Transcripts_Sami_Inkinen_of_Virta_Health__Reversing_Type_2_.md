Please enjoy this transcript of  (https://tim.blog/2026/05/20/sami-inkinen/) my interview with Sami Inkinen ( (https://x.com/samiinkinen) @samiinkinen), a Finnish-born, Stanford-trained entrepreneur and the founder and CEO/president of  (https://www.trulia.com/) Trulia and  (http://www.virtahealth.com) Virta Health. Virta is on a mission to reverse metabolic disease in one billion people using technology, AI, and nutrition. He is the founder of  (https://www.fatchancerow.org/) Fat Chance Row, which he created to raise awareness of the dangers of sugar and its connection to diabetes, rowing 2,750 miles from California to Hawaii with his wife—completely unsupported—while breaking a world record in the process.
