NVIDIA Wins COMPUTEX 2026 Best Choice Awards for Innovations Spanning AI Factories, Robotics and Autonomous Vehicles 

NVIDIA Vera Rubin NVL72, NVIDIA Jetson Thor and NVIDIA Alpamayo were honored across four categories at Asia’s premier technology and computer trade exhibition.

At this year’s COMPUTEX Best Choice Awards (BCA), NVIDIA today received honors recognizing its innovation in AI computing, integrated circuits and autonomous vehicle (AV) development.

The  (https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/data-center/vera-rubin-nvl72/) NVIDIA Vera Rubin NVL72 rack-scale AI supercomputer won a Golden Award and the Sustainable Tech Special Award; the  (https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/autonomous-machines/embedded-systems/jetson-thor/) NVIDIA Jetson Thor platform for edge AI and robotics won a Golden Award; and the  (https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/solutions/autonomous-vehicles/alpamayo/) NVIDIA Alpamayo open platform for AV development won the Vehicle Technology and Smart Cockpit Category Award.

Entries were evaluated on their functionality, innovation and market potential, showcased at the premier computer and technology trade exhibition.

Jensen Huang, founder and CEO of NVIDIA, will deliver a  (https://www.nvidia.com/en-tw/gtc/taipei/keynote/) keynote at COMPUTEX on Monday, June 1, at 11 a.m. Taipei time.
