## Inhalt

try Appshots in the Codex app: Anthony Kroeger (@kr0der) Codex just launched one of the coolest features - Appshots. by pressing both CMD keyboard buttons, context of whatever app you're on gets sent to Codex, including screenshots and text. you might be thinking "isn't this the same as screenshotting and pasting it", and no it isn't. Appshots also pass in context from the app that isn't visible on the screen. For example if you're viewing a Google Doc and take an appshot, it passes in text that's scrolled offscreen as well. along with this release, OpenAI made remote Codex still work even while your laptop screen is locked, so you can leave your laptop on 24/7 at home, safely, while you code from your phone. highly recommend trying out the Codex app - so many features that don't exist elsewhere yet and the app is just delightful to use — http://nitter.perennialte.ch/kr0der/status/2057728283010306227#m
