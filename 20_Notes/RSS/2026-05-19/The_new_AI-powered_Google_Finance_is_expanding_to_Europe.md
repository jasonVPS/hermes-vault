This week, the  (https://www.google.com/finance/beta/) new, AI-powered Google Finance is launching  (https://support.google.com/websearch/answer/16490185?hl=en#zippy=:~:text=Google%20Finance%20availability) across Europe, with full local language support. This reimagined experience offers a suite of powerful capabilities designed to help you better understand the financial world.

AI-powered research: Ask about anything, from individual stocks to broader market trends. You’ll get a comprehensive AI response, with links to learn more. For more complex questions, try  (https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/search/new-google-finance-ai-deep-search/) Deep Search, now globally available in Google Finance.

Advanced visualizations: New charting tools let you go beyond basic historical performance. View technical indicators, like moving average envelopes, or tap  (https://x.com/thefox/status/2051700174582653351) key moments on stock charts to learn why the price changed that day.

Real-time intel: A revamped news feed and expanded data for commodities and cryptocurrencies keep you informed as markets move.

Live earnings: Follow corporate earnings calls with live audio, synchronized transcripts and AI-generated insights, including annotated highlights that help you hone in on what matters.
