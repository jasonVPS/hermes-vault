Transcript

The future of war has been evolving before our eyes in Ukraine, yet the west still plans to fight the last war. In this special episode, guest host  (https://open.substack.com/users/8243895-noah-smith?utm_source=mentions) Noah Smith ( (https://x.com/noahpinion) @noahpinion) and  (https://open.substack.com/users/94300760-brandon-anderson?utm_source=mentions) Brandon Anderson sit down with Yaroslav Azhnyuk ( (https://x.com/YaroslavAzhnyuk) @YaroslavAzhnyuk), a serial tech founder who went from building  (https://petcube.com/) PetCube to founding  (https://thefourthlaw.ai/) The Fourth Law, one of the world’s most advanced AI-guided drone companies. Over two hours we cover the technology, tactics, and geopolitics of drone warfare, and why the modern battlefield has already left the West behind:

Yaroslav’s personal history and the Ukraine war [00:01:04 – 00:14:01]

The modern drone tech stack: why FPV drones are the new god of war, the future of the rifleman, fiber optic vs. AI, five levels of autonomy, and the eight dimensions of the autonomous battlefield [00:14:01 – 01:05:13]

The geopolitics and economics of drones: China’s manufacturing advantage, the drone race, Western defense readiness, countermeasures, and why the gap is widening [01:05:13 – 01:58:57]

For those looking for  (https://open.substack.com/users/8243895-noah-smith?utm_source=mentions) Noah Smith’s commentary, it really gets going around the 00:51:31 mark.
