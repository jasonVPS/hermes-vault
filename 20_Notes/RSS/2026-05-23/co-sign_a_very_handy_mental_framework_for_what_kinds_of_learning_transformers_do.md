co-sign. a very handy mental framework for what kinds of learning transformers do well today, and why it runs into limitations. when @ankit2119 and i wrote about the need for adversarial world models earlier this year, we were describing a couple of the functions of these rungs of thinking that bring us ever closer to the kolmogorov-limit generator of reality. throwing more params, more power, more everything at a demonstrably inefficient paradigm will be outclassed by the simple solution that can hypothesize and seek truth rather than backfit a house of cards - although the bitter lesson is it is simpler to scale and we may hit agi anyway because human intelligence just isn’t that smart nor plentiful Rishabh Agarwal (@agarwl_) Very well written blog.
