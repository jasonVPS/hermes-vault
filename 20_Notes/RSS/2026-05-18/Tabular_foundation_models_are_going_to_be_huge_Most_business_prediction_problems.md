Tabular foundation models are going to be huge. Most business prediction problems live in messy tables, not text or clean time series. 2 examples: - A health tracking app predicting tomorrow's recovery score, or whether you'll wake up sick. Inputs: sleep, HRV, heart rate, skin temp, training load, age. Goal: sleep better, train smarter, dodge the flu. @WHOOP and @ouraring do versions today, but every team in the space stood up its own ML stack. - Ecommerce operators forecasting weekly unit demand per SKU. Inputs: price, promos, seasonality, marketing spend, inventory. Stakes: working capital trapped in dead inventory, end-of-season markdowns, customers lost forever to a stockout. Today it's gut feel, or a senior data scientist who can't iterate fast enough. A tabular foundation model collapses "6 weeks to build, retrain monthly" into testing and shipping in a week. Synthefy-Tabular is the first to actually clear the bar. SOTA on a 100-dataset regression benchmark, 3 to 5x smaller than other SOTA models, with a "thinking mode" that reasons about its own context before predicting. Foundation models are expanding from "AI for text, images, video" to "AI for data". Much wider surface than it sounds. Somi Agarwal (@somi_synthefy) When we started Synthefy, our north star was simple: build foundation models that let anyone, not just ML engineers, make accurate predictions about the real world. Migas 1.0 proved small, open time-series models could compete with the giants. Migas 1.5 went further, becoming the first TSFM to fuse text + time-series for context-aware forecasting. Today, with Synthefy-Tabular, our first tabular foundation model, we're going beyond forecasting entirely. Demand, revenue, risk, churn, pricing, conversion. Most of the prediction problems our customers care about don't live in clean time series. They live in messy, structured data: high-frequency trading data, transaction logs, supply chain demand, insurance claims, ad-spend attribution, energy load curves. Synthefy-Tabular is built to solve all of it, out of the box, with state-of-the-art accuracy on a ~100-dataset regression benchmark, while being 3x to 5x smaller than every other SOTA tabular model. We're especially excited about thinking mode. Letting the model optimize its own context before it predicts. We believe this is what unlocks the next leap in reliability for predictions in production. Stay tuned for the complete release. Full model code, Hugging Face weights, and the technical report are coming soon. 🚀 — http://nitter.perennialte.ch/somi_synthefy/status/2054003243349598270#m
